**Description:**
Super Notes is a fast plain-text scratchpad for the Mac. Press ⌥A anywhere and start typing.

The first word of a note decides what it does:
• list — checklists with checkboxes, bullets and numbering
• math — arithmetic with units and currencies, running totals with sum, avg and count
• timer 25m — countdowns and pomodoros with notifications
• code — syntax-coloured snippets

Also: search across every note, undo history per note, The Void for deleted notes, automatic backups, Vim mode,
14 themes, notes locked behind Touch ID, export to Markdown, PDF, plain text, Apple Notes, Obsidian and Bear, and
end-to-end-encrypted sync through your own iCloud Drive.

`::` commands: built-in commands for dates, JSON, regex, text case, templates and more — and you can write your
own in JavaScript. On-device AI (`::ask`, `::summarize`, `::rewrite`, `::translate`) runs on your Mac with Apple's
model on macOS 26, or an optional 1.3 GB open model you choose to download. Nothing you write leaves your Mac.

No account. No analytics. Works offline.
